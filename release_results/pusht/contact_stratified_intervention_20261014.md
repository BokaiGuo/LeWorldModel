# PushT contact-stratified intervention — 2026-10-14

Protocol: root-disjoint `free`, `approach`, `normal_contact`, and
`tangential_contact` strata; 64 train roots and 32 held-out roots per stratum;
paired equal-norm `+a/-a/zero` interventions; horizons 1, 2, and 4.

The compact result is a mechanism diagnosis:

- contact geometry improves the privileged adapter mainly in free-space and
  approach states;
- normal-contact and tangential-contact within-mode action effects remain
  difficult to identify;
- mode observability and within-mode action identifiability are therefore
  distinct bottlenecks.

This is privileged simulator-state adapter evidence. It is not frozen-LeWM
latent causality, RGB-only online planning, or a universal world-model result.
