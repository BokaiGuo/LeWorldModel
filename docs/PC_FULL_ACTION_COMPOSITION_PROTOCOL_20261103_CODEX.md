# Full-action composition: exact-dynamics development protocol

New development version. The four previously observed witness chains are not
used. No prior artifact, admission gate, or stopped PointMaze mechanism is
changed. This known-dynamics experiment cannot establish RGB performance.

## Task and actions

Use the existing two-step unicycle: the first action has bounded velocity
`[-1,1]` and turn `[-pi/2,pi/2]`; the second has bounded velocity `[-1,1]`.
The final turn has no effect on the terminal position and is fixed to zero.
Thus each prefix proposal may contain both translation and turning; proposals
are finite, not an exhaustive covering of the continuous action domain.

Before outcomes, define 24 goals at radii `0.55, 0.8, 1.05` and angles
`2*pi*(j+0.123)/8`, `j=0,...,7`, from initial state `(0,0,0)`. These are
synthetic development tasks, generated by geometry, not prior optimizer
failures or an independently sourced confirmation panel.

## Four methods, common outcome and accounting policy

Every method has the same 2-step action domain and objective, with eight
starts and L-BFGS-B. Staged methods freeze each of eight proposed full-action
prefixes and optimize its scalar suffix. `flat` optimizes all three effective
action coordinates jointly from eight starts. The best evaluated chain is
selected by its actual complete-chain objective.

* `spectral`: around nominal full program `(v0,turn,v1)=(0,0,0.5)`, estimate
  the 2-by-2 finite-difference terminal-position response to first-step
  velocity and turn, then compute a damped inverse response using SVD.
  Generate eight bounded prefixes at scales `[-2,-1,-0.5,-0.25,0.25,0.5,1,2]`
  of the resulting task-residual step.
* `response_no_svd`: identical nominal program, finite-difference response,
  damping and scales, but solve the same regularized linear system directly
  without an SVD. The two generators should be numerically equivalent; this
  is a necessary SVD ablation, not a claim they are distinct search methods.
* `random`: eight independently uniform bounded first-action prefixes, seed
  `2026110900 + root_index`.
* `flat`: the same seeded eight random prefixes and the same initial suffix
  velocity 0.5, but optimizes all effective actions jointly.

Damping is `1e-4`. Each finite-difference arm charges its five two-step
model evaluations (nominal plus four centered perturbations) against the
same per-method model-evaluation cap. Each optimizer objective-and-gradient
call costs one evaluation; allocate remaining evaluations equally over
eight starts in stable order, remainders first. An early-converged start's
unused evaluations are not reassigned. Budgets are 24, 48 and 96; separately
record analytical gradients, dynamics transitions, SVD calls, planning wall
time, and unused evaluations. These heterogeneous counts are not asserted to
represent equal FLOPs. The flat baseline may use all the objective budget;
neither privileged diagnostics nor previously saved good chains are starts.

For each selected staged chain, record immediate goal-distance change,
the absolute final distances under its selected suffix after the selected and
zero prefixes, and each suffix's change relative to the respective zero
suffix. Analytically optimize a bounded suffix separately after each of the
two prefixes and report both distances as a task diagnostic, not optimizer
performance or a substitute for a real RGB intervention.

## Decision rule

After all 24 tasks and all three budgets, pause this spectral construction
unless its mean final distance is lower by more than `1e-9` than both no-SVD and flat
on at least two of the three prespecified budget points. This is a development
stop rule, not a significance test or a scientific gate. Even if it passes,
require task-relevant actual response change, independent roots, fair stronger
baselines and visual confirmation before any PC2/PC3 claim. Random, no-SVD,
and flat results are all retained regardless of outcome.
