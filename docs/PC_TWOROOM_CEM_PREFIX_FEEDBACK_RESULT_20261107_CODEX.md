# Fixed-tail RGB reobservation: development result

For 40 exposed roots and each of two fixed prefixes, the simulator executed
the first ten actions, and the frozen model rescored the same CEM and
previously selected B tails using the actually observed prefix-end RGB and
the remaining 15-step horizon. All 80 choices were sealed before opening the
previous physical outcomes. Prefix traces matched those outcomes at the
declared tolerance. Each pair used two model chain evaluations and six model
packets; this is additional cost on top of the original CEM.

| Prefix | Original CEM-tail success | Rescored choice success | Choices switched to B |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original selected | 39/40 | 39/40 | 4/40 |
| Reversed | 34/40 | 36/40 | 4/40 |

The rescoring corrected **2 of the 7** previously observed
CEM-fail/B-success pair cases; five remained failures. The two arms share
roots and are not independent tasks. The B tail happened to succeed in all
80 prior comparisons; the candidate pool was already exposed and the B tail
was selected before this diagnostic, not generated by the feedback step.

These outcomes are read from previously recorded full-chain replays after
the prefix RGB was actually observed; the chosen continuation was not newly
executed in this experiment. Thus this is a fixed-pool counterfactual, not
continuous closed-loop or independent evidence. It does not override earlier
negative feedback-interval results, prove net value after full costs, or
change PC0–PC5. The model used its existing single-frame context rather
than privileged physical state; the environment reset and assessment used
physical truth. Two tests passed for deterministic selection and invalid
scores. Gym emitted the existing observation-type and action-casting warnings.
