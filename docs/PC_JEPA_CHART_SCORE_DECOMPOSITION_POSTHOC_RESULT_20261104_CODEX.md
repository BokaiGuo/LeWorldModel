# JEPA two-axis chart: saved-bank score decomposition

On the 56 saved planner-sampled chains from one exposed contact context,
the two chart axes account for approximately 45.0%–92.6% of each
predicted full latent MSE; the remaining score lies outside those two axes
under the worker's declared orthonormal chart construction. Pairwise
ordering by chart distance and full MSE agrees for 1,131/1,540 pairs
(73.4%). These pairs are dependent and are not independent tasks.

On the separately labeled eight privileged witness-derived variants the
chart/full ordering agrees on 25/28 pairs. These variants were not
available to the planner. Agreement with actual terminal-encoder scores
is also included in the raw result, but many actual scores tie in the
planner subset; it does not by itself establish physical success or cause
of planning failure.

This is arithmetic on saved neural predictions from one root and the
worker's chart definition, not a fresh neural execution or numerical
reconstruction of the unsaved chart basis. It establishes that the
two-dimensional projected squared distance is not identical to the full
latent objective on this bank. It does not establish that the chart caused
the physical failure, that full latent gradients would improve planning,
or that PC1/PC3 pass or fail generally.
