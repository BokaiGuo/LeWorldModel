# v21 multi-chain refinement: exact-Wall development protocol

v19 refines only the best scored complete chain. v21 instead divides the
remaining full-chain refinement samples as evenly as possible among up to
four distinct seeds: the global scored incumbent and retained final-beam
chains. Every seed was already scored, each restart retains its own
incumbent, and the global best remains eligible. This is a new development
hypothesis; no additional prediction-token budget is granted.

Compare v17, v19, v21 and four-start shared-bank CEM on eight new tasks:
start `[-0.5,y,theta]`, `y` in `{-0.33,0.33}`, `theta` in `{-1.07,1.07}`;
goal `[0.5,gy]`, `gy` in `{-0.19,0.19}`. Check exact tasks against the
enumerated earlier panels; broader provenance remains unknown. Shared
global proposal bank, legal action domain, 15-step horizon, cap of 4,095
prediction tokens and final nonlinear score. Count all response, successor,
refinement and final comparison calls.

Seal all raw action/feature/score traces. Audit the shared bank, complete
action replay with alternate same-environment geometry, token accounting
and final incumbent. Report actual restart counts and allocation, terminal
distance and success `<0.05`. Equal prediction tokens do not establish
equal hardware cost, and this is neither a PC2 intervention nor an
independent PC3 test.

Exactly eight attempts per arm, including failures. Do not retry, replace
roots, adjust thresholds or expand this panel after inspection.
