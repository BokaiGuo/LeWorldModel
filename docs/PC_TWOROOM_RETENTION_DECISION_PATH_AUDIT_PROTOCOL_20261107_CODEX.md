# TwoRoom retention decision-path audit

This retrospective audit uses the saved 40-root timed-prefix RGB development
trajectories. It does not tune a new method or establish physical counterfactual
effects. For each strategy and clock, count direct fallback, selected source,
and retained indices. For program decisions verify selection among all recorded
parent, probe and tail scores and verify the stated retention rule. Count
within-decision nonzero centered descriptor dimensions for two probes.

Compare vector versus lookahead only at identical root and iteration when
recorded RGB/goal/history receipts, old-tail fingerprint, candidate seed, clock,
and parent/probe candidate fingerprints agree. Report all such pairs as well as
the subset with differing retained sets; compare full chosen actions and the
executed packet separately. Subsequent decisions from divergent trajectories
are not considered paired. Per-decision comparisons are descriptive, not
independent task samples or causal success estimates.
