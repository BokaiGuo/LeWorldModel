# New Wall plan-then-feasibility development result

Four new exact-Wall tasks were planned before privileged witnesses were
constructed. Both arms used the same global candidate bank and 1,920
five-step prediction tokens per task. Their saved selected terminal
distances were:

| Root | v13 | Same-bank full-action CEM | v13 success | CEM success |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0.502197 | 0.500833 | no | no |
| 1 | 0.502354 | 0.013205 | no | yes |
| 2 | 0.065168 | 0.033338 | no | yes |
| 3 | 0.500324 | 0.502813 | no | no |

Success uses the existing terminal-distance `< 0.05` threshold. Thus this
small development panel gives 0/4 v13 and 2/4 same-bank CEM, not an
independent statistical comparison or a claim about all CEM configurations.
Equal prediction tokens do not equalize response extraction, decomposition,
or total runtime cost.

After plans were sealed and audited, the prescribed privileged geometric
constructor found two legal 15-step witnesses for each root: **8/8** had
terminal distance below 0.05, with maximum distance approximately
`1.12e-7`. Witnesses required 11–14 non-padding steps. Actions and paths
were checked with both the original exact dynamics and the alternate
same-environment geometry. These witnesses establish that these four tasks
are feasible under the declared action domain and horizon; they are not
planner inputs, method outcomes, or PC2 mechanism evidence.

The plan audit checked all saved scored actions and endpoints, matching
initial banks, prediction-token ledgers, and the scored complete-chain
incumbents. It is an implementation consistency check, not independent
physical validation. The panel is exposed development data and its broader
history independence is unknown. The predeclared stop was reached: do not
rerun, replace roots, or tune on this panel.

PC2 remains unestablished because this panel did not perform the
predeclared final-chain mechanism intervention. PC3 remains unestablished
because this is neither an independent confirmation nor a comprehensive
strong-baseline and total-cost comparison.
