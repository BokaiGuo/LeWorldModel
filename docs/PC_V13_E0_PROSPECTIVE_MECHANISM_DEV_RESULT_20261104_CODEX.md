Applying its actual selected suffix reaches 0.01562, a change of -1.19424
relative to its zero-tail outcome. Applying that same suffix after zero,

difference scales. Both facts independently prevent the full
predeclared intersection. The zero and greedy comparisons satisfy the
base rule, including the local response/gradient and branch checks.

advantage.
