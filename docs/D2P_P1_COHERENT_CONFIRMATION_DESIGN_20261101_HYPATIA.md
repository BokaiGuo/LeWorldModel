# P1 coherent prefix + real-RGB own-suffix: prospective confirmation design

Status: **proposed and not yet frozen for a prospective run**. This contract
uses the coherent method developed after observing earlier outcomes. It is a
prospective test on roots independent of that development history, not an
independently developed method and not proof that roots were absent from model
training. P1 remains closed; this document does not change any existing gate,
native success rule, model, normalizer, action domain, or stopping rule. Under
the user's authorization the main thread may choose and freeze the prospective
contract, source records, and analysis parameters before new outcomes open.
The original paired contract requires advance specification, not a newly
invented independent custodian, reviewer, or run-authorizer. Independent
review is useful where available but is not imposed here as an original gate.
There is no root selection or experiment execution in this document.

## 1. Scientific object and existing evidence

The frozen candidate is the coherent structured prefix followed by a
prefix-specific, real-cp5-RGB H3 structured suffix plan, executed open-loop
for the remaining 15 actions. The current known-root result
([result](D2P_P1_COHERENT_RESULT_20261101_MAIN.md),
[coherent control](D2P_P1_COHERENT_CONTROL_PROTOCOL_20261101_MAIN.md),
[own-suffix protocol](D2P_P1_COHERENT_OWN_SUFFIX_20261101_CODEX.md)) reports
one development root, no native successes, and no complete-cost strong-baseline
comparison. It motivates, but cannot validate, this prospective design.
Fixed common-tail consequences, own-suffix performance, and complete-controller
performance are distinct questions. The paired interpretation and original
gate remain authoritative
([contract](D2P_P1_PAIRED_INTERPRETATION_CONTRACT_20261030_MAIN.md)).

The hypothesis is that, without meaningful immediate task progress, a
model-selected prefix produces a task-relevant change in the consequences of
a common future action and supports a better independently planned suffix than
controls. Neither one of these latter findings alone establishes the whole
mechanism. Object immobility is not sufficient: pusher movement and passive
motion can matter. Real RGB-Q, predicted Q and physical task metrics must not
be conflated.

## 2. Source-qualified cohort; no outcome selection

The proposed fixed sample is **32 distinct source-qualified development-disjoint
roots**, one target/condition per root, and no optional expansion, replacement
after outcome access, or interim efficacy analysis. This number is a resource
proposal, not a power guarantee. Approve it and the detectable effect/power
calculation before the prospective outcome run; if infeasible, revise and
record the design before outcome access, rather than shrink it mid-run.
No rule here asserts 32 is sufficient for the existing P1 gate.

The main thread can produce a candidate manifest without reading any
new model score, action, rollout, success, reward or outcome. Qualification is
limited to:

1. A verifiable source authority and exact environment/version, renderer,
   action chart, target, reset/warmup/history and original task evaluator.
   The root and goal are physically valid and within the original source
   distribution and action/observation domain. Eligibility tolerances, including
   initial geometric position, rotation, collision/penetration and image
   bounds, must be copied from the native source contract or fixed explicitly
   before the candidate manifest is inspected. No latent score or estimated
   method difficulty is an eligibility feature.
2. Canonical identity hashes and source lineage show no overlap with all
   documented P1 development, tuning, calibration, prior evaluation, or
   opened-outcome roots, including aliases, same reset/warmup histories and
   duplicate tasks under translations or other source-recognized equivalences.
   Compare source identifiers and canonical physical/geometric identity,
   not merely seed numbers. Require a completeness audit of the exclusion
   registry; if lineage or alias equivalence cannot be established, quarantine
   the candidate. A different seed, target image, or hash alone is not proof.
3. Goal and history are authorized by the source; availability of a known
   successful action, a physical witness, or a model-selected prefix is
   neither an eligibility criterion nor planner input. Source geometrical
   coverage is reported without tailoring it to earlier root outcomes.

Before model evaluation or physical branching, sort eligible candidates by
SHA-256 of a sealed canonical root identity with a precommitted public salt;
take the first 32 and seal the ordered manifest, exclusion reasons, source
bytes and salt. Ties are broken by canonical identity bytes. The salt is fixed
before viewing the candidate roster; no reroll. If fewer than 32 qualify, do
not start this proposed confirmation cohort; obtain a source-qualified pool under
the same rule and document any dated amendment before outcome access.
Source-only pre-enrollment invalidity may be
resolved before sealing the final roster; after sealing, all 32 remain in the
denominator, including failed or inaccessible roots. Do not use seed shopping,
observed outcomes, candidate quality, native success, or apparent mechanism
to admit, reject, replace or reorder a root.

The roots are **independent of documented development use**, conditional on
the registry audit and actual source sampling. They need not be statistically
independent merely because they are distinct: identify shared source episode,
scene, or generation family in advance. If independent sampling at the
root-family level cannot be documented, root-level inference below is not
confirmatory under the proposed root-level model; prespecify an appropriate
family-level or finite-frame analysis before outcome access, or report only
the supported descriptive result. This is an inference boundary, not a bar
to source auditing or CPU implementation. Training-dataset overlap is unknown
unless separately demonstrated; no training-independence or out-of-training
generalization claim is permitted.

## 3. Frozen arms and paired execution

Before outcomes, hash-bind checkpoint and source, runner, dependencies,
normalizers, goal/Q construction, source authority, render and dynamics,
random seeds, candidate-bank generator, tie rule, costs, and full ordered
schedule. The coherent protocol sums the five per-step Jacobian contributions
for each constant packet, not their average; the underlying full Jacobian is
still calculated and charged. Preserve the original raw-action domain,
prefix protection, candidate ordering, six full-action updates, replay,
selection and deterministic tie behavior. No tuning on these roots. A
baseline's method may differ, but all configurations and budgets must be
frozen before outcomes.

For each root, prepare the identical actual reset, target, two warmup action
packets and RGB history. Generate and seal the entire coherent initial bank
and complete model scores before any physical branch. The primary paired
roles are:

| Role | Prefix5 | cp5 suffix15 |
|---|---|---|
| S | frozen coherent model-selected prefix | own H3 structured plan |
| G | first argmin of predicted one-packet goal Q over the exact S initializer bank; same tie rule | own H3 structured plan |
| Z | five exactly zero physical actions | own H3 structured plan |

G is **same-bank model-greedy**, not a globally optimal or physically greedy
controller. Verify exact initializer packet identity, score every one of the
eight packets and charge its actual computation. Record duplicate prefix
identities and retain all logical roles; S=G is a zero pairwise contrast, not
evidence of a distinct non-greedy choice. Never choose a prefix using its
physical outcome. A stronger baseline B is mandatory for the separate
full-method comparison in §6; its exact planner is an unresolved blocking
choice for claiming a complete fair full-method comparison. Its absence does
not block implementing or validating the S/G/Z and intervention machinery.

Each role replays the exact root and its prescribed prefix in the real
environment. The own-suffix planner receives its own actual cp5 RGB encoding,
the last two original warmup latents, the actual last warmup packet and actual
prefix packet under the original action normalizer, and the same fixed goal/Q.
Physical state is an identity receipt only, never planner input. Use the same
H3 candidate construction, six updates, action constraints, RNG policy and
caps for every role. The incumbent is exactly that role's actual prefix plus
15 zeros, not a previously selected or retrospectively successful plan. Seal
candidate arrays, model scores, argmin and selected suffix before physical
suffix dispatch. Each suffix is an open-loop 15-action proposal; do not call
it closed-loop feedback after cp5.

For the common-tail intervention, independently reset/replay S, G and Z
prefixes and execute (i) the **S-model-selected suffix sealed before any
physical suffix outcome**, and (ii) zero15, giving six logical branches per
root. In addition execute the three own-suffix branches, for nine logical
branches per root; if S own tail is identical to the sealed common tail it may
be physically deduplicated only after exact identity/replay verification,
with logical aliases and full accounting retained. Branch selection cannot
depend on any physical outcome. A common S-origin tail is deliberately
asymmetric and diagnoses effect modification, not optimal reachable-set
capacity. The complete B comparison is additional and cannot be substituted
by these branches.

Recreate rather than set-state unless state restore has been separately
validated against the source contract. Before every suffix verify cp0 identity and the
corresponding prefix's cp5 physical state, dynamics, RGB, action history and
target against its own first receipt. Record all actions and every native
stop, step count, success, frame, and failure; never synthesize a missing
fixed-horizon endpoint. Use authenticated source hashes and durable
non-overwriting records before and after work. If identity, safety, numerical,
source, or accounting checks fail, preserve the branch and stop under the
incident rule; never silently rerun selectively.

## 4. Metrics and mechanism conjunction

Let `E_r,p,t` denote the unchanged native jointXY task error (smaller is
better) for root r, prefix role p and physical step t; report object position,
wrapped angle, pusher error, native reward and native success separately at
0, 5, 10, 15 and 20 when observed. Record units and the native evaluator
version. Also record predicted initial-to-cp5 Q and actual encoded RGB-Q on
the **same actions and times**, separately from physical progress. Do not
count cp5 images, action candidates or branches as independent roots.

All tolerances below are explicit pending values, not inferred from the known
root. The main thread must specify their numerical units, measurement precision
and justification prospectively before the relevant outcomes open. These are
proposed study parameters, not values already required by an existing P1 gate.

* **Non-immediate progress N(r):** the actual S prefix does not improve
  native jointXY by more than `epsilon_E` relative to its own cp0, AND does
  not improve over the corresponding Z prefix's cp5 by more than
  `epsilon_Z`: `E_S,5 - E_0 >= -epsilon_E` and
  `E_S,5 - E_Z,5 >= -epsilon_Z`. Also require the unchanged original
  non-greedy model/physical gate, if it specifies a stricter comparator,
  margin or directional condition. Report S-versus-G cp5 and all decomposed
  metrics, including pusher, without calling component stability full task
  nonprogress. These proposed operational conditions do not replace that gate.
* **Common-tail effect M(r):** with `T` the presealed S-origin tail and `0`
  the zero tail, define `I_r(p) = E_r,p+0,20 - E_r,p+T,20`. Require
  `I_r(S)-I_r(G) >= delta_M` and `I_r(S)-I_r(Z) >= delta_M`, with
  `delta_M > 0` fixed in native units. Report all six absolute endpoints
  and both difference-in-differences, not merely the interaction. This is
  controlled effect modification by a common action; it does not establish
  intrinsic reachability or necessity of the prefix.
* **Own-suffix O(r):** require actual `E_r,G-own,20 - E_r,S-own,20 >= delta_O`
  and `E_r,Z-own,20 - E_r,S-own,20 >= delta_O`, with `delta_O > 0`
  fixed in native units, under the same cp5 optimization opportunity. Report
  native success and all absolute endpoint components separately. The
  prefix-specific tails may differ; this is a paired plan-quality/physical
  outcome test, not the common-tail intervention or a complete-cost win.

The root-level candidate mechanism indicator is **C(r)=N(r) AND M(r) AND
O(r)**, plus any stricter unchanged original requirements. It is zero if
fully observed conditions fail; it is *unknown*, not zero or success, if a
required fixed-time measurement is missing. Report N, M, O and C separately
and their joint overlap, including S=G and both positive and negative effects.
For example, favorable marginal mean effects in different roots do not imply
any root satisfies C. A preselected S tail and own-tail may coincide: preserve
this dependence and do not claim independent mechanism replications.

## 5. Prospective inference, missingness and decision

The following tests, alpha split, `pi0` and n=32 are **design proposals**, not
existing P1 gates. The main thread may replace them with a justified,
prospectively frozen analysis under user authorization; no test may be chosen
or modified using its new outcomes. The unit of analysis is one sampled root family with all its
paired role and branch records; root is the unit only if §2's family condition
holds. Before enrollment, seal a positive minimum relevant conjunction rate
`pi0`, relevant effect margins, precise eligibility tolerances, sample size,
and a power calculation for the fixed n=32. Inference is conditional on the
documented sampling frame; it cannot establish generalization to an undefined
population. No post-hoc subgroup selection.

The proposed **joint-mechanism test** is one-sided exact binomial on the number
of roots satisfying C against `H0: Pr(C) <= pi0`, alpha 0.025. This tests the
conjunction *within each root*, so correlated comparisons and dependence among
N/M/O and between G/Z are preserved rather than treated as independent
trials. Report the exact one-sided 97.5% lower confidence bound for Pr(C),
the full denominator, each component and every uncertainty. This inference
requires independent identically sampled root families; hash ordering alone
does not establish that. If the frame is finite or families are dependent,
the main thread should specify an appropriate finite-frame or family-level
analysis before outcome access. Otherwise report the limitation and descriptive
results rather than claim this binomial inference.

The second co-primary is a **full-method B comparison**: one-sided exact
paired sign test of native success difference S versus B at alpha 0.025, using
only discordant root pairs and counting ties as ties; report the success
difference and a prespecified paired 95% confidence interval, and its
discordant counts. This tests direction conditional on discordance, not a
clinically meaningful effect size or superiority for jointXY. S and B
execution must be complete-cost/feedback matched as in §6. No discordance
means no positive finding. With only 32 roots this may have low power: seal a
simulation/power calculation under plausible discordance and effect sizes
before outcome access, and report inconclusive if underpowered or unresolved.
The two co-primary one-sided tests use a Bonferroni split of 0.05; require
both to reject for the proposed combined empirical finding. This is an
additional conservative study rule, not a replacement for existing P1 gates.
Secondary S-G and S-Z paired continuous contrasts should be reported with
root-family cluster bootstrap intervals, using the whole vector of all arms
and tails per resampled family; exploratory p-values, if any, use a
predeclared Holm family. No inference from treating arms as independent.

For early native success/termination report the actual observed success at
its real time; fixed step-20 jointXY is missing if not observed, not copied
forward. Report domain failures, compute failures, invalid identity, aborts,
and missing frames separately by root and role. For the conjunction test,
give a conservative lower bound by treating every unknown C as failure and
an upper bound treating every unknown C as success; only the lower-bound
analysis may support the positive finding. For paired success, do not
silently discard missing pairs: use worst-case assignment against S for a
conservative primary analysis, and present complete-pair and best-case
sensitivity separately. A non-success error is not a documented native
failure. Binding/source violations invalidate the affected receipt and may
invalidate the whole study; do not convert them into valid negative outcomes.
Safety-driven early stopping is allowed but cannot be replaced by another
root or used to manufacture a fixed-horizon measurement. Predefine
administrative stop and incident adjudication before launch.

Even if both statistical criteria pass, report only the prespecified
prospective result conditional on source, validity and matching. P1 still
requires separate adjudication against every original gate, original
mechanism definition, strong baseline, independent-source and provenance
requirements. Failure to reject is not evidence of equivalence.

## 6. Full costs and strong-baseline contract

Publish per-root/per-arm **attempted and completed**, separately:

* RGB image encoder calls and images, goal/Q construction and encodings,
  shared and per-arm; full candidate initialization and score replay;
* predictor and action-encoder calls **and sample/packet dimensions**, reverse
  passes, JVP/VJP directions, full Jacobian construction, SVD, linear solves,
  optimization iterations, selection evaluations, failed attempts and retries;
* common preprocessing, model loading, checkpoint/normalizer I/O, actual
  GPU memory, measured synchronized wall time and, if instrumented, energy
  and FLOPs; explicitly mark unknown rather than infer FLOPs from call counts;
* physical reset, warmup, prefix, cp5 encoding, own suffix, every diagnostic
  common/zero tail, replays, failures and safety stops, including the cost of
  establishing identity. Separate deployable-controller cost from extra
  evaluation/intervention overhead, but report both.

Declare shared costs in a dedicated ledger and allocate the entire shared
generation cost to each comparator in the conservative per-method fairness
view as well as once in the experiment total; never treat a shared bank as
free for one arm. Failures are charged, not refunded. Same local H3 node
budget (historically 390 predict, 240 backward, 81 replay/selection per node,
plus separate encoding) is not proof of equal total compute. Establish actual
instrumentation and vector caps from the frozen implementation, including
all initialization and G's eight score calls, before running.

B must be a preselected, genuinely competitive, source-valid planning
baseline, not only Z, random, or the library-restricted G. Give B identical
task information, frozen checkpoint where applicable, action domain, reset,
15-step suffix horizon, native stopping rules, cp5 real-RGB opportunity, and
access to no more or less real feedback than S; compare complete prefix plus
suffix controller cost under a common multi-axis cap or a predeclared
equal-wall-clock budget with independently recorded vector usage. Both
methods may stop early without forced waste, and B may use the full permitted
budget. A single scalar call count does not establish equality across
encoder, forward and backward work. If cap feasibility, measurement,
hardware isolation or matching cannot be verified, the full-method comparison
is descriptive only and its co-primary cannot pass. Diagnostic branches are
not charged as deployment costs, but must be disclosed in total experiment
costs; no post-hoc favorable budget point.

## 7. Readiness and executable next steps

The original paired contract requires metrics, tolerances, resource caps,
sample size and failure treatment to be frozen before confirmation outcomes;
it does not prescribe the proposed numerical values, test, alpha split or
additional personnel. Under the user's authorization, the main thread can
carry out the following:

1. **Source work, actionable now:** inventory the existing source authority,
   root identities and all documented development/previously opened roots;
   implement and CPU-test geometry/source-only qualification, alias checks,
   ordered sampling and exclusion records without opening candidate outcomes.
   Missing lineage, unresolved aliases, insufficient qualifying roots or
   unauthenticated source bytes are real limitations for a development-disjoint
   claim; report exactly what is missing. No training exclusion is inferred.
2. **Parameters, actionable now:** derive native precision and geometric
   predicates from the source; propose and freeze justified `epsilon_E`,
   `epsilon_Z`, `delta_M`, `delta_O`, `pi0`, n, sampling rule, inference and
   power/precision calculation before new outcome access. These are choices
   for this proposal, not pre-existing P1 thresholds. Preserve every existing
   gate; if a proposal conflicts with one, do not relax the existing gate.
3. **Implementable engineering, actionable now:** choose and specify B and
   its hyperparameters, seed policy, common multi-axis caps, instrumentation,
   feedback matching, paired interval and incident rules. Implement and
   CPU-test selection and same-bank identity, reset/cp5 receipt binding,
   durable charging, early stop, tamper rejection, and paired/conjunction
   analysis. Match claims to verified costs; an unavailable or unmatched B
   blocks the fair full-method claim, not all preparation.
4. **Freeze and run only when ready:** record actual source/checkpoint/renderer/
   code hashes, source-closure limitations, clean output paths, ledger schemas
   and capacity; date and hash the contract, exclusions, manifests and seeds
   before new outcome access. Follow the user's authorization and any
   applicable operational permissions for CUDA/physical execution. Synthetic
   fixtures are not experiment results. Changes made after outcome access
   cannot retroactively make those outcomes prospective confirmation.
