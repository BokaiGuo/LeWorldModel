This read-only checker joins the existing bridge's explicit prepare, report, completion, MAIN full receipt and sealed source SHA through the existing Stage B `build`; it does not call the old full consumer. It compares the latent bits to the receipt task, runs the new owned projection and checks its five contexts against the original historical executor inputs. SHA is byte authentication against the supplied pinned inputs, not proof of their authority.

The CLI is **historical bridge ABI only** because Stage B pins the original bridge source/path and producer schema. It inherits MAIN's completed receipt; it does not reconstruct the complete native event/physics/model proof, independently establish that latent came from the encoder, certify future source selection or trusted time, or authorize dispatch. New future producers need their own reviewed receipt/source closure and executor ABI. Previous results are known, not prospective or fresh. All scientific flags remain false.

Fixture tests use fabricated tiny physical metadata and temporary PNG/NPY, not production evidence. No neural or physics operation or upstream full strict is performed.
