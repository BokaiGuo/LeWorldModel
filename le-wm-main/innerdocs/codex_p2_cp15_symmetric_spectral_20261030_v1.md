their transfer and joint parent prefixes. For each base, bind the actual saved
control-15 feedback context, original goal, exact 15-step physical prefix and
task/root identity. Both arms start from the same all-zero 30-logit H3 suffix,

of radius .75*sqrt(15). Arms are joint full-variable spectral updates and
successive suffix-freezing spectral updates; this is a constrained packet
construction comparison, not a demonstrated reusable-program composition.

action-encoder and predict calls, 2 full 192x30 Jacobian requests, 2 SVDs,

inherited, not free or a new actual-online evaluation. No invented balancing
computations or outcome-based selection. Root 1's two bases have identical
feedback contexts and must not be counted as independent observations.
